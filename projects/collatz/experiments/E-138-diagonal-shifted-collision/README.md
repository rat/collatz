# E-138: diagonal term of the shifted-collision decomposition

Related hypotheses: H-157 (q-adic logarithmic renormalization), H-156,
H-155, H-159 (arithmetic transfer of the iid tail), H-161 (same affine map,
different question).

## What this tests

Write `mu_(q,ell)` for the Syracuse law of parameter `q` with geometric
weight `P(a)=2^-a`, and `K_(q,ell) = q^ell sum_x mu_(q,ell)(x)^2`.

Condition on the first exponent. With `F_ell = 2^-a (1 + q F_(ell-1))` and
`Y = 1 + q F_(ell-1) mod q^ell`, two independent copies collide exactly when
`Y' = 2^(a'-a) Y`. The exponents are iid geometric on `{1,2,...}`, so
`P[a'-a = s] = 2^-|s| / 3` and

```text
K_(q,ell) = (q/3) sum_(s in Z) 2^-|s| H_(ell-1)(s),
H_(ell-1)(s) = q^(ell-1) P[Y' = 2^s Y mod q^ell],
H_(ell-1)(0) = K_(q,ell-1).
```

Each `H` is a probability times a positive constant, so every term is
nonnegative. This is the Fourier dual of H-156 and H-157: the transform of
`2^-|s|` on the subgroup generated by `2^d` is exactly the multiplier
`W_(q,d)` of H-157, and `q/3` is its circle average. What the time-domain
form adds is termwise nonnegativity, which the spectral form hides inside a
signed balance.

Dropping every `s != 0` gives the unconditional rate

```text
K_(q,ell) >= (q/3) K_(q,ell-1)    for every ell >= 1.
```

The inequality already holds at `ell=1`, where `F_0 = 0` and `Y = 1` is
deterministic, so `H_0(s) = [d | s]` and the identity gives the closed form
`K_(q,1) = (q/3) (2^d+1)/(2^d-1)`, checked against the law for every `q`
tested. With the base `K_(q,0) = 1` (trivial law modulo `q^0 = 1`),
iterating gives `K_(q,ell) >= (q/3)^ell`.

Three lines, no Parseval, no orbit structure, and no maximal-lifting
hypothesis `v_q(2^d-1)=1`. Since `c_(q,d) = (q/3) (2^d-1)/(2^d+1) < q/3` for
every `d`, this is a strict improvement on H-157's constant, and it also
covers the exceptional (Wieferich) primes and composite odd `q`, both of
which H-157 excludes. It stays inside H-157's own bracket
`[c_(q,d), C_(q,d)]`, so it contradicts nothing there.

## Scripts

```sh
python3 diagonal_rate.py            # identity and the q/3 rate, several q
python3 q3_shifted_profile.py       # q=3: the increment E_ell, and min over affine states
python3 affine_transfer.py          # exact affine transfer operator, and a failed certificate
```

`diagonal_rate.py` builds the law by the plain convolution
`mu_ell(y) = sum_(a>=1) 2^-a nu(2^a y)`, with no assumption on the orbit of
2, so composite `q` and non-maximal lifting are both admissible inputs.

## What comes out

`diagonal_rate.py`, for `q` in `{3,5,7,9,11,13,15,21,25}` up to modulus
2e6: the identity holds to `2.3e-13` absolute at worst, and the ratio
`K_(q,ell)/K_(q,ell-1)` never falls below `q/3` (smallest observed excess
`8.0e-9`, at `q=25` where the first nonzero off-diagonal shift is `s=20`).
The ratio decreases towards `q/3` from above, so the bound is not just
valid, it is asymptotically sharp:

```text
q=5  (q/3=1.666667, H-157 gives 1.470588):  1.7238, 1.6962, 1.6814, ..., 1.6688 at ell=8
q=7  (q/3=2.333333, H-157 gives 1.814815):  2.4187, 2.3701, 2.3463, ..., 2.3352 at ell=6
q=11 (q/3=3.666667, H-157 gives 3.659512):  3.6671, 3.6667, 3.6667, 3.6667 at ell=5
```

For `q=3` the rate is `q/3 = 1`, so it yields monotonicity of `K_ell` and
nothing more. Monotonicity is already known (H-155 writes the increment as a
sum of squares, H-140 records it as a martingale second moment), so at `q=3`
the diagonal term is not new information.

`q3_shifted_profile.py` gives what is new at `q=3`, the explicit form of the
increment. With `G_(ell-1)(r) = H_(ell-1)(2r)`,

```text
E_ell = K_ell - K_(ell-1) = 2 sum_(r>=1) 4^-r G_(ell-1)(r) >= (1/2) G_(ell-1)(1),
G_(ell-1)(1) = T_(ell-1)(4,1),   T_ell(u,v) = 3^ell P[F' = u F + v mod 3^ell].
```

Measured:

```text
ell   K_ell      E_ell      G(1)      G(2)      G(3)     (1/2)G(1)
  4   3.068646   0.464214   0.70303   0.49512   1.33461   0.351515
  8   4.931742   0.465921   0.71709   0.47605   1.24238   0.358544
 12   6.802899   0.468941   0.72427   0.47333   1.23741   0.362133
```

So the missing uniform margin for `q=3` reduces to one arithmetic
statement: a level-uniform positive lower bound on the single affine shifted
collision `T_ell(4,1)`. No cancellation is involved, only positivity. The
observed value rises slowly through `0.724` at `ell=12`; `0.5 * 0.72` would
give `K_ell >= K_1 + 0.36 (ell-1)`.

The same script scans every affine state. Over all units `u` and all `v`,

```text
ell        1        2        3        4        5        6        7        8
min T   0.3333   0.2449   0.2137   0.2023   0.1961   0.1930   0.1915   0.1911
```

The minimum decreases and flattens near `0.191`, which is evidence, not
proof, that `inf_ell inf_(u,v) T_ell(u,v) > 0`. That statement alone would
close the `q=3` margin.

## Two routes that failed

`affine_transfer.py` validates, in exact rational arithmetic at levels 1, 2
and 3, the level-independent recursion

```text
T_ell(u,v) = 3 sum_(a,a'>=1) 2^-(a+a') T_(ell-1)(w, (w + v 2^a' - 1)/3),
w = u 2^(a'-a),   over the pairs with 3 | (w + v 2^a' - 1),   T_0 = 1.
```

Route A, induction on `ell`. The operator is monotone, so `T_2 >= T_1`
everywhere would give `T_ell >= T_1(4,1) = 2/3` for all `ell` and close the
question. It is false: 22 of the 54 states mod 9 have `T_2 < T_1`, for
instance `T_2(1,1) = 24/49 < 2/3`. The target state itself is fine
(`T_1(4,1) = 2/3`, `T_2(4,1) = 34/49`), the induction is not.

Route B, a subinvariant certificate at finite resolution. A function `h` of
`(u,v) mod 3^k` with `0 <= h <= 1` and `(Th) >= h` on every lift would give
`T_ell >= h` by induction from `T_0 = 1`. The largest such `h` is the limit
of `h <- min(h, T_* h)` from `h = 1`, where `T_*` minimizes over lifts. It
collapses geometrically: about `0.79` per round at `k=1`, about `0.88` at
`k=2`. Refining `k` improves the rate but the structural reason for the loss
does not go away, since `min` over lifts lets an adversary pick the worst
lift again at every level with no consistency between levels, which the real
dynamics forbids. No certificate of this shape exists at `k=1` or `k=2`.

## What this does not show

`K_ell -> infinity` is equivalent to the density martingale being unbounded
in `L^2` (H-155), which rules out an `L^2` density. It does not by itself
prove that the limiting measure is singular; that would need `L^1`
non-uniform-integrability, which is not established here.

The `q/3` rate holds for every odd `q`, prime or composite. The step from
divergence to "no `L^2` density" goes through H-155's Parseval decomposition,
stated for prime modulus, and it was not checked whether its
character-conductor bookkeeping survives at `q^ell` with a non-cyclic unit
group. The `L^2` conclusion is therefore scoped to prime `q`; the rate is
not.

For `q=3` nothing is proved beyond monotonicity, which was already known.
The reduction to `T_ell(4,1)` is a reformulation, and the `0.191` floor is a
finite-level measurement over eight levels.

## Relation to O1 and H-161

The map `A(k) = 4k+1` and the recursion `W = N + (1/4) W circ A` used in
H-161 are the same objects that appear here: `G_ell(1)` is the
nearest-neighbour correlation `(1/M) sum_j N_j N_(j+1)` along exactly the
orbit that E-132 measures. The two questions are related, not identical.
E-132 measures how often two consecutive positions are jointly small, and
finds anti-clustering; what the margin here needs is a lower bound on the
expectation of their product. Anti-clustering pushes in the helpful
direction for that bound without supplying it.
